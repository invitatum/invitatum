# Invitatum — Brand & Design System Direction

## Visual Language & Identity
- **Personality**: Romantic, Floral, Elegant, Modern, Minimalist, Premium, Soft, and Clean.
- **Audience**: Indonesian and International couples planning Weddings & Engagements who seek sophisticated, thoughtful digital invitations without clutter or tacky animations.
- **Dial**: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2
  - Energy: Balanced, warm romantic luxury, warm welcoming tone.
  - Rhythm: Balanced section cadence with alternating asymmetrical floral motifs, arched frames, and editorial typography.
  - Motion: Smooth romantic reveal, wax-seal opening, subtle floral parallax, strictly respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Color Palette (Anti-Slop WCAG AA Calibrated)
- **Ivory (Base Background)**: `#FAF7F2` (soft cream), `#FFFFFF` (card surfaces)
- **Dusty Pink (Romantic Accent)**: `#D99B9B` (primary soft rose), `#8C3B4A` (deep rose for AA text contrast)
- **Sage Green (Botanical Accent)**: `#607755` (nature/foliage), `#384732` (deep olive for AA text contrast)
- **Champagne Gold (Luxury Accent)**: `#C5A059` (metallic accent), `#8C6E2D` (gold for dark contrast)
- **Charcoal / Deep Earth (Body Text)**: `#242625` (contrast ratio > 11:1 against Ivory)
- **Muted Earth (Subtitles/Secondary Text)**: `#5A605B` (contrast ratio > 5.2:1 against Ivory)

## Typography
- **Serif (Headings & Names)**: Serif display fonts (Cormorant, Playfair, Cinzel inspired) for couple names, quote headers, and romantic milestones.
- **Sans-Serif (Body & UI Controls)**: Clean geometric sans (Jakarta Sans, Inter style) for buttons, forms, RSVP fields, and table data.

## Spacing & Components
- Minimum tap target: 44px on mobile.
- Section spacing: Generous whitespace (`py-16 md:py-24`).
- Rounded corners: `rounded-2xl` for soft cards, `rounded-full` for badges and circular floral frames.
- Shadows: Soft organic diffused shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`), strictly avoiding harsh dark drops.
- Card & Layout rhythm: Arched photography frames, wax-seal interactive buttons, elegant RSVP modals.
